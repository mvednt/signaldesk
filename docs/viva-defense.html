SignalDesk Viva Defense Sheet
B2B Account Signal Monitor · oral defense prep

Answers you can say out loud.

Lead with the decision, then the reason. Offer to run a proof whenever you can.

The 30-second opener ~35 sec

SignalDesk tells Arjun which of his ten accounts are worth a call this week, and why. Twice a week it scans live news for each account, uses Gemini Flash to judge whether anything is a real workforce-management buying trigger, scores and ranks what it finds, and emails him a Hot List with a drafted outreach angle. The one rule it never breaks: it writes only its own signal columns and cannot touch Arjun's notes — and that's enforced by the database itself, not just by the code.

01

Core & architecture

CORE

In one line, what does it do?

Say

It's a timing tool. Arjun's problem isn't finding accounts, it's knowing which ones have a reason to be contacted right now. SignalDesk watches live news for all ten, detects buying triggers, and surfaces only the warm ones twice a week with the reason attached.

FLOW

Walk me through the pipeline.

Say

Five stages. Fetch live articles per account; normalize and de-duplicate them on a content hash; match each one to the right account; send the recent matches to Gemini to classify and explain; then score deterministically and write the results. Each stage is its own module, and the scoring part is pure — no database, no AI — so I can test it in isolation.

DATA

What are your sources?

Say

Google News RSS is the primary source — it's free, keyless, and works from any server, so it's safe in production. NewsAPI is layered on as optional enrichment. Both feed the same pipeline, so adding a source is one new adapter function.

02

The preservation guarantee

KEY

How do you guarantee you never overwrite Arjun's notes?

Say

This is the requirement the brief bolds twice, so I enforced it at the deepest layer. His data lives in its own tables — account_crm and contact_history. The system writes as a dedicated Postgres role, signal_writer, and that role has REVOKE ALL on those two tables. So the protection isn't "the code is careful" — the database physically refuses any write to his columns. On top of that, every write uses an explicit column allowlist, never a blind upsert of a whole object.

Proof move

Offer to run preservation_proof.sql live: as signal_writer it attempts to edit Arjun's note, the DB returns permission denied (42501), and the note comes back unchanged.

WHY

Why two layers? Isn't the role enough?

Say

The role grant is the real guarantee; the allowlist is defense-in-depth. If someone later gave the role broader permissions by mistake, the explicit column lists still mean the application code never names a human-owned column to write to. Two independent things would both have to fail.

03

The AI layer

MODEL

Which AI, and why not Claude?

Say

Every runtime AI call is Gemini Flash — that's a hard constraint in the brief, and I kept to it. Claude was only the builder, never in the running system. Gemini returns structured JSON against a fixed schema, so I get back typed fields, not free text I have to parse.

TRUST

How do you stop it inventing triggers?

Say

Three guards. The prompt forces it to use only facts stated in the title and snippet — no inferring. If the item isn't a genuine workforce trigger it must return is_relevant=false with an empty angle. And everything comes back through a strict schema I validate with Zod, with confidence clamped to zero-to-one. If the output doesn't fit, it's rejected, not trusted.

Proof move

In the demo, Gemini flagged "Zepto Cafe cuts footprint after expansion" as a real operational trigger and correctly rejected food-safety stories as not workforce-relevant.

SECURITY

What if an article contains instructions aimed at your AI?

Say

The prompt treats the article text as untrusted data and is told explicitly never to follow any instruction inside it. The news snippet is content to analyze, never commands. So a planted "ignore your rules" line in an article body does nothing.

04

Scoring & ranking

RANK

How do you rank accounts — does the LLM decide?

Say

No, and that's deliberate. Ranking is a deterministic formula so it's explainable and I can calibrate it. An account's score is its strongest signal scored as recency times trigger-type times source quality times match confidence times the AI's own confidence, on a 0-to-100 scale. Leadership changes weigh highest, then headcount. The LLM contributes the category and a confidence; it never sets the rank directly.

DECAY

How do quiet accounts fall down the list automatically?

Say

The recency factor decays to zero over a 30-day window. As a signal ages, that factor shrinks, so the account's score drops on its own — no one has to mark it cold. If nothing new comes in, it sinks naturally. And because scores live in system-owned tables, that decay never touches Arjun's own priority or notes.

05

The Hot List

EMAIL

What's in the email, and when does it send?

Say

Monday and Thursday mornings, 7am India time. It's the top ten accounts with the strongest signals from the last three days — each with a one-line signal summary and a Gemini-drafted outreach angle that ties the trigger to what Arjun sells. It goes out through Resend, and every send is logged.

EDGE

What if fewer than ten accounts are warm?

Say

It sends fewer. The three-day window is strict and it never pads the list with stale or invented accounts. In the live demo only Ola Electric fell inside the window, so the preview returned exactly one — that's the system being honest, not broken.

SAFETY

Could it send twice or create duplicates?

Say

No. The notification table has a unique constraint on date-plus-kind, so a second send on the same day is blocked by the database. Signal ingestion is idempotent the same way — a unique content hash with on-conflict-do-nothing — so re-running a job never double-writes.

06

Deployment & live proof

LIVE

Is it actually deployed with real data?

Say

Yes — public URL on Vercel, serving real scored signals right now. Three warm accounts this cycle: Ola Electric, PhonePe, Zepto, each with a detected trigger and a drafted angle. The other seven are correctly quiet. The data is live from Google News, not seeded fixtures.

Proof move

Have the live URL open in a tab before you walk in. Showing beats describing.

07

The caveats — handled, not hidden

SEC

Is the signal_writer password secure? It looks like a demo value.

Say

It's still the seeded value, and that's a deliberate call. The boundary doesn't rely on that password being secret — it relies on table grants. signal_writer has REVOKE ALL on Arjun's tables, so even someone holding the exact password and logging in directly cannot write to his CRM. The database rejects it. Rotating would be good hygiene, but it changes nothing about the security boundary.

If pushed

"I can rotate it in two lines — alter the role password, then update the connection string on Vercel. I left it because the protection sits one layer deeper, at the grant level. The principle: never trust a secret to do a job a permission can do."

OPS

Will the twice-weekly job finish in time for all ten accounts?

Say

On Vercel's free Hobby plan a single run is capped at 60 seconds. One account takes about 33 seconds because of the news fetch plus the Gemini calls, so ten in one sequential pass could approach that cap. The failure mode is graceful, not destructive: every write is idempotent, so a run that gets cut short just rolls the remaining accounts into the next run. Nothing corrupts, nothing double-sends.

If pushed

"The fix is to batch accounts across runs so no single run nears 60 seconds — it's in my deployment notes. On the Pro plan the cap is minutes, so it's a free-tier limit, not an architectural one. One honest detail: my code requests a 5-minute limit, but Hobby overrides it to 60, so I don't rely on that line."

SCOPE

The brief names LinkedIn posts as a source. Where are they?

Say

I cover press releases and news articles through Google News and NewsAPI. LinkedIn I left out on purpose — there's no compliant public API for post content and scraping it violates their terms. Rather than ship something brittle or against ToS, I covered the sources I can get reliably and live. The architecture is source-agnostic, so a compliant LinkedIn feed would be one new adapter into the same pipeline.

If pushed

"In source channels, yes, one's missing — but the triggers the brief actually cares about, headcount, leadership, funding, digitalisation, operational news, nearly all surface in news and press coverage too. I'm capturing the signals, just not that one channel."

08

Lightning round

What's the stack?
Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Supabase Postgres, Gemini Flash, Resend, on Vercel.
How many accounts?
Ten, seeded with real Indian companies like Delhivery, Zepto, and PhonePe.
What counts as a trigger?
Headcount expansion, a new CHRO or VP Ops, funding, digitalisation, or an operational challenge.
Why multiply the factors instead of add them?
So one weak factor drags the score down — a stale or low-confidence signal can't rank high.
30-day decay but 3-day Hot List — why two windows?
The dashboard score reflects standing momentum; the Hot List is a strict twice-weekly alert.
Who can write Arjun's tables?
Only an authenticated Arjun session — the automation role and anonymous readers both can't.
Who can read the dashboard?
Anyone, read-only through the anon key, enforced by row-level security.
What if Gemini returns malformed JSON?
Zod rejects it and the signal is skipped, never stored half-formed.
What if a news source is down?
NewsAPI fails silently to an empty list; Google News RSS is the floor.
How do you avoid duplicate signals?
A content hash per article, unique in the database, with on-conflict-do-nothing.
Is the email itself written by AI?
No — the layout is fixed; only the one-line outreach angle per account is Gemini-written.
How are AI costs bounded?
At most six Gemini calls per account per run, and Flash is cheap.
Does "morning" mean the server's morning?
No — run dates are computed in Asia/Kolkata, so it's Arjun's Monday and Thursday.
What's the Components Map?
The required architecture diagram showing data flow and the human-versus-system boundary.
How do you know it works end to end?
A verified production run: ingest wrote as signal_writer, the Hot List emailed via Resend, CRM untouched.
How to carry yourself

Every caveat is understood and bounded. Lead with the decision, show the proof when you can, and let the database do the talking on the one rule that matters most.
